About HENNGE

HENNGE is a B2B SaaS company based in Tokyo Japan, and we are at the forefront of the changing workplace. HENNGE One, one of the top cloud security services in Japan, allows customers to adopt flexible work environments and safely harness the power of the cloud from any location. With cloud security ensured, employees are free to focus on working smarter, improving quality, and achieving business results. For further details regarding the engineering teams, products, and more, please visit this link.

Position

We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to take ownership of backend and web-platform development for HENNGE One, our flagship cloud-security suite serving over 2.8 million users globally. As a B2B SaaS leader in Japan, we provide mission-critical security solutions that empower enterprises to work safely from anywhere

 

In this role, you will join the Secure Transfer Team, responsible for the development of HENNGE One Data Loss Prevention (DLP) Edition. You will be developing our high-security file exchange solution—the "Secure Vault" of our ecosystem. Your mission is to architect the fault-tolerant, high-availability systems required to handle and protect sensitive enterprise data. This position is ideal for engineers who want to bridge the gap between advanced backend development and deep cloud infrastructure, utilizing tools like Kubernetes and Terraform to ensure data integrity.

 

While this role primarily focuses on the DLP Edition, we value engineers who are flexible and open to new challenges, with opportunities to move between teams and products through two-way discussions that support your growth and the company’s goals.

 

Tech Stack:

FastAPI, Kubernetes, ArgoCD, PostgreSQL, AWS

 

What you’ll do

  • Design, build, and maintain new features for HENNGE One, using Python and other web technologies.
  • Architect scalable, fault-tolerant services deployed in cloud environments
  • Automate deployment, testing, monitoring and documentation via CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code.
  • Collaborate closely with Product Management, Design, and Customer Success teams to deliver high-quality user experiences.
  • Participate actively in code reviews, discussions and technical decision-making.
  • Mentor junior engineers, and contribute to improving team processes and engineering culture

Your qualification

Minimum Qualifications:

  • 3+ years of experience developing production-scale web applications using Python.
  • Strong experience across the software development lifecycle (SDLC), from system design to deployment and maintenance.
  • Hands-on experience with containerization and orchestration technologies (e.g., Kubernetes, ArgoCD).
  • Experience with Infrastructure-as-Code tools, such as Terraform.
  • Deep expertise in architecting and operating mission-critical AWS environments.
  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and clean-code practices.
  • Ability to lead technical initiatives, mentor junior engineers, and work collaboratively in a team environment.
  • Fluency in English (Japanese is not required), equivalent to CEFR B2 level or above (e.g., TOEIC 785+).
  • Interest in contributing to open-source projects or participating in the tech community.

 

While not required, we’d love to hear if you have:

  • Degree in Computer Science or related technical field.
  • Experience building large-scale distributed systems or SaaS products.
  • Familiarity of Go, or a strong interest in learning it, to help build and maintain our multi-language backend infrastructure.
  • Additional experience with cloud security best practices, including PII obfuscation and secure data-handling protocols.
  • Team leadership experience.

What we offer

  • English Allowance and Advanced Language Expert Allowance: JPY 10,000 ~ JPY 130,000 per month, paid alongside base salary, if the employee fulfills the requirements.
  • Physical Communication Bonus: JPY 4,000 per day for coming to the office and interacting with other employees, paid out as bonus according to the number of days. Details will be provided at the offer meeting.
  • Transportation allowance (maximum of JPY 35,000 per month).
  • Employee Stock Ownership Plan.
  • Insurance: Health, Employment, Industrial Injury, Welfare pension.
  • Opportunities to attend international and domestic conferences.
  • Financial support for technical certifications and technical learning programs.

 

Reasons why you will enjoy working at HENNGE

  • We Celebrate Diversity

Join a vibrant team from over 20 countries, united by a love for technology and Japanese culture. Located in Shibuya, Tokyo, we offer a dynamic, non-traditional work environment in one of the world's most exciting cities.

  • Smooth Japan Relocation

We make your move to Japan smooth and stress-free. We provide comprehensive support such as flight tickets, visa sponsorship, and Japanese lessons, to ensure a smooth onboarding process for our new hires, ensuring a seamless transition and a warm welcome for all candidates.

  • Balanced Work Life

Work-life balance is highly valued at HENNGE, and lots of fun happens at our office! 200+ of social channels in our Slack, from weekly board game nights to snowboarding club, internal and seasonal events, monthly tech sessions with beer bashes, attendance of international tech conferences, and more. Employees have access to a flexible working hours system (with core working hours) to accommodate their preferred working style.

  • Passion for Technology

Our CEO has an engineering background and remains deeply engaged in product development. This hands-on understanding of technology helps shape a culture where thoughtful design, technical quality, and continuous improvement are genuinely valued. For engineers, this means working in an environment led by someone who understands the challenges of building products and actively supports teams in doing their best work.

  • Collaborative Teams

Work in autonomous teams where your decisions shape our services. Influence every aspect of our products, from UX and architecture to development and deployment, in an agile and collaborative setting.

Hiring process

If you are also interested in the Senior Software Engineer, Backend / Web Dev – HENNGE Access Control position, you do not need to submit applications for both roles. Since both positions share the same hiring pipeline, one application is sufficient. Please simply indicate your interest in both roles in your cover letter.

 

1. Solve our online coding challenge

Complete our online coding challenge here:

https://challenge.hennge.com/?entry-code=RELOCATE-PYTHON-SR. You will receive an email with our guidance upon your submission, and there is no time limit to finish the challenge.

Please note that your application will be automatically rejected if:

  • you have fewer than three years of professional Python experience, and/or
  • you complete the coding challenge using a programming language other than Python.

2. Upload your CV and Cover Letter. Please check our guide on how to prepare them here: https://hennge.com/global/lifeathennge/article/202403-crafting-the-perfect-cover-letter.html

3. Casual interview with our HR

4. 2 (or 3) interviews with our engineers (no live coding)

5. Interview with our CEO

 

We value your time and want you to perform at your best, so the coding challenge will be the only technical assessment in the process. There will be no live coding sessions.

Have more questions? Please consult our FAQ.
