CAREER

HENNGE offers a unique work environment that is different from typical Japanese offices. We are proud to be international, flexible, and casual. English is our official language, and for our Cloud Product Development Division Engineering roles, Japanese proficiency is not required.



What matters most is a shared passion for technology. If open-source software excites you and you love to learn, we invite you to join us in Tokyo!
